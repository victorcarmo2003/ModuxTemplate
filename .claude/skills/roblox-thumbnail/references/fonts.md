Do **not** use: Burbank Big Condensed (Fortnite's, paid per device), Gotham (paid), Impact (commercial rights
unclear even though Windows ships it), Obelix Pro (personal-use only on some sites).

Per-genre default: simulator → Luckiest Guy yellow→orange gradient + black stroke; cute → Fredoka/Lilita white +
brown/dark stroke; action → Bangers tilted −8°, white + black + red shadow; horror → Creepster or plain Titan One
in cold white / red with thin black stroke + cyan/red glow instead of white outer stroke.
